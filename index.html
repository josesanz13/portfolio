Context

A payments platform moving real money between banks, wallets and merchants in Colombia. Every minute of downtime costs money and trust.

What I built

  • Backend and infrastructure for peaks of hundreds of thousands of requests per minute.
  • A modular, DDD-oriented architecture using Strategy and Factory patterns to plug in each financial provider without touching the system core.
  • Secure REST integrations with traditional and alternative financial providers.
  • Asynchronous processing with queues split by callback type and worker reconfiguration to remove bottlenecks.
  • Optimization of multi-million-row transaction tables with an index strategy and zero-downtime rollout plans.

Result

Service stayed up through traffic peaks, response times held steady, and infrastructure audits (critical logs, OOM control, Nginx/PHP-FPM tuning) became routine instead of emergency work.

Node.jsTypeScriptPostgreSQLRedisAWS EC2 · S3 · Route 53DDD / Strategy
